<!-- Overview -->
<section class="section">
  <div class="container">
    <h2 style="margin-bottom:16px;">Overview</h2>
    <p style="color:var(--gray-600);line-height:1.8;">Solar photovoltaic inverters convert DC power from solar panels into grid-synchronized AC power. The DC-link aluminum electrolytic capacitor is one of the most critical components, performing input ripple smoothing, energy buffering, and supporting the inverter's MPPT (Maximum Power Point Tracking) algorithm.</p>
    <p style="color:var(--gray-600);line-height:1.8;margin-top:12px;">Solar inverters face unique challenges: wide daily temperature cycling (rooftop installations can reach &minus;25&deg;C at night and +85&deg;C during the day), 10&ndash;15 year expected service life, and high-frequency switching ripple from the MPPT and inverter stages. Capacitor failure is the leading cause of solar inverter downtime, making correct selection essential.</p>
  </div>
</section>

        <p>Solar inverters are expected to operate for 10&ndash;15 years without maintenance. Capacitor life must be calculated at actual operating temperature, not just rated temperature.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">⚡</div>
        <h3>Low ESR at High Frequency</h3>
        <p>Modern solar inverters switch at 16&ndash;50 kHz. Low ESR at these frequencies reduces internal heating and improves inverter efficiency.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">💧</div>
        <h3>Moisture Resistance</h3>
        <p>Outdoor installations expose capacitors to high humidity and condensation. Robust seal design prevents moisture ingress that accelerates electrolyte degradation.</p>

        <p style="color:var(--gray-600);font-size:0.92rem;line-height:1.7;">Smooths the rectified PV input and provides energy storage for the MPPT stage. This is the largest capacitor in the inverter and the most critical for reliability.</p>

        <p style="color:var(--gray-600);font-size:0.92rem;line-height:1.7;">Filters high-frequency switching harmonics from the inverter output before the grid connection. Lower capacitance values but high ripple current requirements.</p>
      </div>

        <p style="color:var(--gray-600);font-size:0.92rem;line-height:1.7;">Active PFC boost circuits require low-ESR capacitors to achieve high power factor and comply with grid harmonic standards (IEEE 519, IEC 61000).</p>
