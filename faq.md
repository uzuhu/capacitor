        </div>

          <p>Our standard product range covers voltages from <strong>6.3V to 500V DC</strong>. For specific non-standard voltage requirements, our engineering team can develop custom-rated capacitors based on your application needs.</p>

      <div class="faq-item">
        <div class="faq-question" onclick="toggleFaq(this)">
          <span>What is the difference between snap-in and screw terminal capacitors?</span>
          <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
        </div>
        <div class="faq-answer">
          <p><strong>Snap-in terminal capacitors</strong> have quick-mount terminals that snap into PCB or chassis mounting clips — ideal for compact designs and automated assembly. <strong>Screw terminal capacitors</strong> use threaded studs or bolted connections, providing superior vibration resistance and higher current handling for heavy-duty industrial applications. Learn more in our detailed comparison: <a href="{{ '/blog/snap-in-vs-screw-terminal-which-to-choose/' | relative_url }}">Snap-In vs Screw Terminal — Which to Choose</a>.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="faq-item">
        <div class="faq-question" onclick="toggleFaq(this)">
          <span>What is the operating temperature range?</span>
          <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
        </div>
        <div class="faq-answer">
          <p>Our standard capacitors are rated for <strong>-40&deg;C to +85&deg;C</strong> or <strong>-40&deg;C to +105&deg;C</strong>, depending on the series. Extended temperature range options are available for extreme environment applications. Contact us with your specific temperature requirements for a recommendation.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="faq-item">
        <div class="faq-question" onclick="toggleFaq(this)">
          <span>Do you offer long-life capacitors?</span>
          <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
        </div>
        <div class="faq-answer">
          <p>Yes. Several of our series are specifically designed for <strong>extended operational life</strong>, with enhanced electrolyte formulations and optimized sealing technologies. These are ideal for applications requiring 10+ years of continuous service, such as UPS systems, solar inverters, and industrial power supplies. See our <a href="{{ '/products/' | relative_url }}">product page</a> for life rating details for each series.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="faq-item">
        <div class="faq-question" onclick="toggleFaq(this)">
          <span>What is high ripple current capability, and which series support it?</span>
          <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
        </div>
        <div class="faq-answer">
          <p>High ripple current capability means the capacitor can handle larger AC current components without excessive internal heating. Our CD294, CD295, CD13HL, and CD13NH series are specifically optimized for <strong>high ripple current applications</strong> such as inverter welding machines, frequency converters, and motor drives. These series use advanced anode foil and low-ESR electrolyte systems.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

    parent.querySelectorAll('.faq-item.open').forEach(function(i) {
      if (i !== item) i.classList.remove('open');
    });
  }
  if (isOpen) {
    item.classList.remove('open');
  } else {
    item.classList.add('open');
  }
}
