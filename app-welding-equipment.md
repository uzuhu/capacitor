<!-- Overview -->
<section class="section">
  <div class="container">
    <h2 style="margin-bottom:16px;">Overview</h2>
    <p style="color:var(--gray-600);line-height:1.8;">Modern inverter welding machines have replaced traditional transformer-based welders with compact, efficient switch-mode designs. Aluminum electrolytic capacitors in welding inverters face extreme operating conditions: very high ripple current during welding arcs, rapid load cycling between open-circuit and short-circuit states, high ambient temperatures in enclosed chassis, and exposure to conductive dust and metal particles in industrial environments.</p>
    <p style="color:var(--gray-600);line-height:1.8;margin-top:12px;">The DC-link capacitor bank must handle the high pulsed current demand of the welding process while maintaining voltage stability. Insufficient capacitance or degraded capacitors result in unstable arc characteristics and poor weld quality.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<!-- Key Requirements -->
<section class="section">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section-title">
      <h2>Key Capacitor Requirements</h2>
      <div class="divider"></div>
    </div>
    <div class="features-grid">
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">⚡</div>
        <h3>Extreme Ripple Current</h3>
        <p>Welding arcs draw very high pulsed currents. The DC-link capacitor must handle RMS ripple currents far exceeding those in typical inverter applications, often 2&ndash;3x the nominal rating.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">🌡️</div>
        <h3>High Ambient Temperature</h3>
        <p>Welding machines are often fan-cooled but still operate in high-temperature environments. The capacitor's hotspot temperature must be carefully calculated considering both ambient and self-heating from ripple current.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">🔄</div>
        <h3>Load Cycle Durability</h3>
        <p>Welding involves frequent start-stop cycles. The thermal cycling from load to no-load conditions stresses the capacitor's internal connections and seal over thousands of operating hours.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">💨</div>
        <h3>Dust &amp; Particle Resistance</h3>
        <p>Welding environments contain conductive metal dust and particles. Capacitors with insulated sleeves and robust terminal insulation are essential for safety and reliability.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
