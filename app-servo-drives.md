<!-- Key Requirements -->
<section class="section section-alt">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section-title">
      <h2>Key Capacitor Requirements</h2>
      <div class="divider"></div>
    </div>
    <div class="features-grid">
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">⚡</div>
        <h3>Low ESR at High Frequency</h3>
        <p>Servo drives switch at 20&ndash;50 kHz, much higher than standard VFDs. Capacitor ESR must be low at these frequencies to minimize internal heating and maintain efficiency.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">🔄</div>
        <h3>Rapid Cycling Capability</h3>
        <p>Servo applications involve frequent acceleration and deceleration. The capacitor must absorb and release energy rapidly without degradation, cycle after cycle, for years of operation.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">📐</div>
        <h3>Compact Form Factor</h3>
        <p>Multi-axis servo systems pack multiple drives in a single cabinet. Capacitors with high capacitance density and compact snap-in mounting are preferred to minimize cabinet size.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">🛑</div>
        <h3>Braking Energy Absorption</h3>
        <p>During rapid deceleration, the motor acts as a generator. The DC-link capacitor must absorb the resulting energy surge, often assisted by a braking chopper circuit.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
