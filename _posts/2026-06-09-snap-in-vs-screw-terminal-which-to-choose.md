> **Summary:** A detailed comparison of snap-in and screw terminal aluminum electrolytic capacitors. Learn which type is best for your application.


Selecting the right **mounting type** for aluminum electrolytic capacitors is crucial for performance, reliability, and manufacturability.

This guide compares **snap-in** and **screw terminal** capacitors to help you choose the best option.

## Quick Comparison Table



|  | Snap-In | Screw Terminal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diameter range | 22–40 mm | 35–100 mm |
| Current capability | Up to ~10A ripple | 10A+ ripple |
| Mounting method | PCB / chassis snap-in | Busbar / heavy-duty screw |
| Assembly | Manual or automated insertion | Screw terminal connection |
| Typical applications | VFD output stage, SMPS | UPS, welding, large inverters |
| HUAYU series | CD17FX, CD292–CD298 | CD92, CD135, CD136 |



## Snap-In Terminal Capacitors

### Advantages
- **Compact design**: Suitable for space-constrained applications
- **Automated assembly**: Compatible with pick-and-place machines
- **Cost-effective**: Lower manufacturing cost for medium-power applications
- **Good ripple current**: Up to 10A RMS at 105°C

### Disadvantages
- **Limited current capability**: Not suitable for very high ripple current
- **Mechanical stress**: Snap-in tabs can be stressed during assembly
- **Size limitation**: Maximum diameter typically 90 mm

### Ideal Applications
- **Variable Frequency Drives (VFD)** up to 50 kW
- **Switching Power Supplies (SMPS)** up to 10 kW
- **LED drivers** for high-power lighting
- **Industrial power supplies** with moderate ripple current

### HUAYU Snap-In Series
- **CD17FX**: Compact series, 22–35 mm diameter
- **CD292–CD298**: Standard industrial series, 22–40 mm diameter
- **Voltage range**: 16V to 500V DC
- **Ripple current**: Up to 10A RMS at 105°C
- **Lifetime**: 5,000 hours at 105°C

## Screw Terminal Capacitors

### Advantages
- **High current capability**: 10A+ ripple current handling
- **Robust connection**: Secure screw terminals for high-vibration environments
- **Large case sizes**: Up to 100 mm diameter for high capacitance values
- **Excellent thermal performance**: Better heat dissipation

### Disadvantages
- **Larger size**: Requires more PCB or enclosure space
- **Higher cost**: More expensive than snap-in types
- **Manual assembly**: Typically requires manual screw tightening

### Ideal Applications
- **UPS systems** for data centers and industrial facilities
- **Welding equipment** with ultra-high ripple current
- **Large inverters** for renewable energy systems
- **Heavy-duty industrial equipment** with high power demands

### HUAYU Screw Terminal Series
- **CD92, CD92L**: General-purpose screw terminal series
- **CD135, CD136**: Ultra-high ripple current series
- **Voltage range**: 16V to 500V DC
- **Ripple current**: 10A+ RMS at 105°C
- **Lifetime**: 5,000 hours at 105°C

## Selection Decision Tree

### 1. What is your ripple current requirement?
- **< 10A RMS** → Consider snap-in
- **≥ 10A RMS** → Choose screw terminal

### 2. What is your power level?
- **< 50 kW** → Snap-in may be sufficient
- **≥ 50 kW** → Screw terminal recommended

### 3. What is your space constraint?
- **Limited space** → Snap-in (more compact)
- **Adequate space** → Either type, prefer screw terminal for high power

### 4. What is your assembly method?
- **Automated assembly** → Snap-in (pick-and-place compatible)
- **Manual assembly** → Either type

### 5. What is your budget?
- **Cost-sensitive** → Snap-in (lower cost)
- **Performance-critical** → Screw terminal (better performance)

## Mixed Configuration Examples

### Example 1: VFD Design (30 kW)
- **DC-link**: 2× CD298 snap-in capacitors in parallel
- **Reason**: 30 kW < 50 kW, ripple current < 10A RMS per capacitor

### Example 2: UPS Design (100 kVA)
- **DC-link**: 4× CD136 screw terminal capacitors in parallel
- **Reason**: 100 kVA > 50 kW, high ripple current requirement

### Example 3: Welding Machine (200A)
- **Energy storage**: 2× CD136 screw terminal capacitors in parallel
- **Reason**: Ultra-high ripple current, robust connection required
