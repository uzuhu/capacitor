At HUAYU, **quality is our top priority**. Every aluminum electrolytic capacitor undergoes **rigorous testing** before shipment to ensure reliable performance in demanding applications.

This article explains our comprehensive **quality control process**.

## Quality Control Flowchart

```
Raw Material Inspection
        ↓
Formation Test (Oxide Layer Verification)
        ↓
Aging Test (Electrical Stabilization)
        ↓
Electrical Testing (100% Testing)
        ↓
Visual Inspection & Dimensional Check
        ↓
Packaging & Shipping
```

## 1. Raw Material Inspection

### Aluminum Foil
- **Thickness measurement** (±1 µm tolerance)
- **Surface roughness** verification
- **Chemical composition** analysis

### Electrolyte
- **Conductivity** testing
- **Impurity analysis** (heavy metals, etc.)
- **Shelf life** verification

### Separator Paper
- **Thickness and porosity** testing
- **Chemical compatibility** with electrolyte
- **Impurity analysis**

## 2. Formation Test

The **formation test** verifies the quality of the aluminum oxide (Al₂O₃) dielectric layer.

### Test Procedure
1. Apply **formation voltage** (1.2-1.5× rated voltage)
2. Monitor **leakage current** during formation
3. Verify **oxide layer thickness** and uniformity
4. **Re-form** if defects are detected (self-healing)

### Acceptance Criteria
- **Leakge current** < specified limit (typically 0.01 CV or 3 µA)
- **Formation time** within specified range
- **No visual defects** (bulging, leakage, etc.)

## 3. Aging Test

The **aging test** stabilizes the electrical characteristics of the capacitor.

### Test Procedure
1. Apply **rated voltage** at **maximum operating temperature** (105°C or 125°C)
2. **Hold for specified time** (typically 2-4 hours)
3. Monitor **leakage current** and **capacitance change**
4. **Cool down** and re-measure electrical parameters

### Purpose
- **Stabilize electrical characteristics**
- **Screen for early failures** (infant mortality)
- **Improve long-term reliability**

## 4. Electrical Testing (100% Testing)

**Every capacitor** undergoes **100% electrical testing** before shipment.

### Test Items

| Test Item | Test Condition | Acceptance Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Capacitance** | 20°C, 120 Hz | Within specified tolerance (±20%) |
| **Tangent of loss angle (tan δ)** | 20°C, 120 Hz | < specified limit (e.g., 0.15) |
| **Leakge current** | Rated voltage, 20°C | < 0.01 CV or 3 µA |
| **Impedance (Z)** | 20°C, 100 kHz | < specified limit |
| **Rated ripple current** | 105°C, 100-120 Hz | Meets specification |

### Test Equipment
- **Automatic test stations** with high accuracy
- **Statistical process control (SPC)** for monitoring
- **Data logging** for traceability

## 5. Visual Inspection & Dimensional Check

### Visual Inspection
- **Surface defects** (scratches, dents, etc.)
- **Terminal/lead condition** (bent, damaged, etc.)
- **Label quality** (correct part number, date code, etc.)
- **Packaging integrity** (anti-static bag, cushioning, etc.)

### Dimensional Check
- **Diameter and length** (±0.5 mm tolerance)
- **Terminal/lead dimensions** (pitch, diameter, etc.)
- **Case shape** (cylindical, no bulging, etc.)
