HUAYU provides **full OEM and ODM services** for aluminum electrolytic capacitors. Whether you need **custom specifications** or **private labeling**, we can manufacture capacitors with your brand.

This article explains our OEM/ODM capabilities and process.

## What is OEM/ODM?

### OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer)
- **Your brand**: Capacitors manufactured with **your company logo and part number**
- **Standard specifications**: Use HUAYU's standard series with your branding
- **Fast turnaround**: Typically 2-4 weeks for first order

### ODM (Original Design Manufacturer)
- **Custom design**: Capacitors designed to your **exact specifications**
- **Non-standard parameters**: Voltage, capacitance, case size, terminal type
- **Engineering support**: Our team helps optimize the design for your application

## Customization Options

### 1. Electrical Specifications
- **Voltage rating**: Custom voltage from 10V to 800V DC
- **Capacitance**: Custom capacitance values and tolerance
- **Ripple current**: Enhanced ripple current capability
- **ESR**: Low ESR options for high-frequency applications
- **Operating temperature**: 105°C standard, 125°C available
- **Lifetime**: Extended lifetime options (10,000+ hours at 105°C)

### 2. Mechanical Specifications
- **Case size**: Non-standard diameter and length
- **Terminal type**: Snap-in, screw terminal (M5, M6, custom)
- **Terminal position**: Custom terminal location and orientation
- **Mounting style**: PCB mount, chassis mount, busbar mount

### 3. Branding Options
- **Label**: Your company logo and part number
- **Printing**: Custom printing on capacitor case
- **Packaging**: Your branding on boxes and anti-static bags
- **Documentation**: Your company information on datasheets and certificates
