Welcome to the official **Nantong HUAYU Electronics Co., Ltd.** website!

## About This Website

This website is built with **Jekyll** and hosted on **GitHub Pages**. It serves as our official platform to:

- Showcase our full range of **snap-in** and **screw terminal** aluminum electrolytic capacitors
- Provide **technical resources** including datasheets, dimension drawings, and application guidelines
- Share **company news** and technical articles via our blog

## How to Read New Articles

> **Simply upload a new `.md` file to the `_posts/` folder — it will appear on the blog page automatically!**

### Steps to Publish a New Article:

1. Create a new file in `_posts/` with the naming format:  
   `YYYY-MM-DD-your-article-title.md`
2. Add the front matter at the top of the file:
   ```yaml
   ---
   layout: post
   title: "Your Article Title"
   date: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS +0800
   categories: [Category Name]
   author: "Your Name"
   excerpt: "A short summary of the article"
   ---
   ```
3. Write your content in **Markdown** below the front matter
4. **Commit and push** to GitHub — GitHub Pages will rebuild the site automatically (takes 1–2 minutes)
