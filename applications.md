      <div class="feature-card" style="border-top:3px solid var(--orange);text-align:center;">
        <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem;margin-bottom:10px;">Voltage Selection</h3>
        <p style="color:var(--gray-600);font-size:0.88rem;line-height:1.7;">Recommended operating voltage is 70%&ndash;80% of the rated voltage, which can significantly extend capacitor service life.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card" style="border-top:3px solid var(--blue-dark);text-align:center;">
        <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem;margin-bottom:10px;">Temperature &amp; Lifetime</h3>
        <p style="color:var(--gray-600);font-size:0.88rem;line-height:1.7;">For every 10&deg;C decrease in operating temperature, capacitor lifetime approximately doubles.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card" style="border-top:3px solid var(--orange);text-align:center;">
        <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem;margin-bottom:10px;">Ripple Current</h3>
        <p style="color:var(--gray-600);font-size:0.88rem;line-height:1.7;">Actual RMS ripple current should not exceed 80% of the rated value. High-frequency applications require reference to the frequency coefficient table.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card" style="border-top:3px solid var(--blue-dark);text-align:center;">
        <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem;margin-bottom:10px;">Capacitance Tolerance</h3>
        <p style="color:var(--gray-600);font-size:0.88rem;line-height:1.7;">Standard capacitance is &plusmn;20% (M grade). Special applications can provide &plusmn;10% (K grade) products.</p>
