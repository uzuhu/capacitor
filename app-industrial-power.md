<!-- Key Requirements -->
<section class="section section-alt">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section-title">
      <h2>Key Capacitor Requirements</h2>
      <div class="divider"></div>
    </div>
    <div class="features-grid">
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">⚡</div>
        <h3>Low ESR</h3>
        <p>Low equivalent series resistance is essential for minimizing power loss and heat generation in high-frequency switching circuits. High ESR leads to excessive internal heating and reduced capacitor lifespan.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">📈</div>
        <h3>High Ripple Current</h3>
        <p>SMPS and PFC circuits generate significant AC ripple on the DC bus. Capacitors must handle the RMS ripple current without exceeding temperature rise limits, typically rated at 105&deg;C.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">🌡️</div>
        <h3>Temperature Endurance</h3>
        <p>Power supplies often operate in enclosed environments with limited airflow. Capacitors must withstand sustained high ambient temperatures while maintaining electrical performance over thousands of hours.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">🔄</div>
        <h3>Long Service Life</h3>
        <p>Industrial equipment runs 24/7. Capacitors with extended life ratings (5,000&ndash;10,000 hours at 105&deg;C) reduce maintenance frequency and total cost of ownership.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
