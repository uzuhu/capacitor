        <h2>Understanding Ripple Current</h2>
        <p>In DC power circuits, a capacitor's ripple current rating determines how much AC current it can safely handle before internal heating exceeds design limits. Excessive ripple current causes:</p>
        <ul style="color:var(--gray-600);margin-top:12px;">
          <li><strong>Internal temperature rise</strong> &mdash; accelerates electrolyte evaporation</li>
          <li><strong>Capacitance loss</strong> &mdash; from electrolyte dry-out over time</li>
          <li><strong>ESR increase</strong> &mdash; further compounding self-heating</li>
          <li><strong>Pressure buildup</strong> &mdash; potentially activating the pressure relief vent</li>
        </ul>
        <p style="margin-top:16px;">HUAYU's high ripple current series use advanced low-ESR electrolyte and optimized internal construction to minimize self-heating and maximize useful operating life in ripple-intensive applications.</p>
