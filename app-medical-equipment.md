<!-- Overview -->
<section class="section">
  <div class="container">
    <h2 style="margin-bottom:16px;">Overview</h2>
    <p style="color:var(--gray-600);line-height:1.8;">Medical imaging and diagnostic equipment &mdash; including X-ray generators, CT scanners, MRI systems, and ultrasound machines &mdash; require power supplies with exceptional reliability and performance. The aluminum electrolytic capacitors in these systems must deliver stable operation over thousands of hours, often in controlled environments but with strict regulatory requirements for safety and electromagnetic compatibility.</p>
    <p style="color:var(--gray-600);line-height:1.8;margin-top:12px;">Capacitor failure in medical equipment can lead to system downtime that directly impacts patient care. This makes capacitor selection, quality assurance, and preventive maintenance planning critical considerations in medical device design.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section section-alt">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section-title">
      <h2>Application Scenarios</h2>
      <div class="divider"></div>
    </div>
    <div class="features-grid">
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">🏥</div>
        <h3>X-Ray Generators</h3>
        <p>High-voltage DC power supplies for X-ray tubes require capacitors with tight capacitance tolerance and low leakage current. Must handle high-voltage pulsed loads during exposure sequences.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">🔬</div>
        <h3>CT Scanners</h3>
        <p>Rotating anode CT systems use slip-ring power transfer with high-ripple DC-link capacitors. Must withstand continuous operation during long scanning protocols and rapid gantry rotation vibration.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">🧲</div>
        <h3>MRI Systems</h3>
        <p>Gradient amplifier power supplies in MRI systems demand very high peak current capability. DC-link capacitors must deliver high ripple current with low ESR for fast gradient switching.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="feature-card">
        <div class="feature-icon">💊</div>
        <h3>Ultrasound &amp; Monitoring</h3>
        <p>Lower power but high-reliability requirements. Capacitors in ultrasound transmitters and patient monitoring equipment must meet medical device safety standards (IEC 60601).</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
